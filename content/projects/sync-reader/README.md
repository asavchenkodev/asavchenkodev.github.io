# Sync Reader

Sync Reader is a parallel text reader for learning German with Russian translations. It lets you read bilingual texts side by side, inspect words in context, save useful vocabulary, and export it for Anki.

[Open the live tool](/tools/sync-reader/)

## Why I built it

The main idea is to see a word immediately in sentence context instead of only seeing a direct translation. You can also read the text first in your native language and then in the language you are learning.

The app stays serverless: no account, subscription, or paid usage limit. Texts, folders, saved words, and study data are stored locally in the browser.

## Features

- Parallel Russian and German reading panes.
- Linked highlighting across both texts: click or hover a word to see its translation and grammar notes.
- Saved vocabulary and Anki-style review.
- TSV export for Anki.
- A local library with folders and browser storage.
- 26 built-in texts at A2 and B1 level on everyday life, money, technology, work, and travel.
- Import of plain-text files generated by an LLM, with problems reported per line.

## How to add your own text

1. Open the tool and choose **Add text**. Describe the topic and level, then copy the prompt into Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.
2. Paste the whole reply back into the app and save it. Each sentence is a `DE:` line and an `RU:` line, and linked words are marked like `[den Schrank]4` with the same number on both sides.
3. To import texts you already have, drop `.txt` files or a whole folder onto the app. A folder becomes a library folder, and any broken lines are listed with their line numbers.
