A post is a folder under posts/ with a README.org (or README.md) inside it.
Images and attachments can sit next to it and be linked with [[file:figure.png]].

Remove the draft tag to publish it. It then appears in posts/ and feed.xml,
and gets its own page at /posts/first-post/.
