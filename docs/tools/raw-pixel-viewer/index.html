Supported formats

View raw pixel files right in the browser. Nothing is uploaded: files are decoded locally. Set width, height, stride and offset, or use Guess format to find them.

YUV semi-planar
NV12, NV21 (4:2:0), NV16, NV61 (4:2:2), P010 (10-bit NV12, MSB aligned), plus 10/12/14/16-bit variants in 16-bit words, little or big endian.
YUV planar
I420 / YUV420P, YV12 / YVU420P, YUV422P, YVU422P, including high bit depth such as yuv420p10le.
YUV packed
YUYV / YUY2 and UYVY (4:2:2). BT.601, BT.709 and BT.2020 matrices, limited or full range.
Bayer RAW
RGGB, BGGR, GRBG and GBRG patterns: 8-bit, 10/12/14/16-bit in 16-bit words, and packed MIPI RAW10 and RAW12, with bilinear demosaic.
RGB and grayscale
RGB24, BGR24, RGBA32, BGRA32, ARGB32, ABGR32 (alpha or padding), GRAY8 / Y8 and GRAY16.
Images
PNG, JPEG and BMP, for comparing a raw buffer with a reference image.

Tools: split, grid, wipe and diff views, linked zoom, per-pixel values, RGB and luma histograms, and PNG or JPEG export.
